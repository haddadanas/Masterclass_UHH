Masterclasses @ UHH (iSpy WebGL)

This repository contains a modified version of the iSpy WebGL Event Display for the CMS Experiment, adapted for the Masterclasses at the University of Hamburg. The original version was developed by F. Ali, L. Barnard, M. Hategan, S. Lee, C. Logrén, T. McCauley, P. Nguyen, M. Saunby.
